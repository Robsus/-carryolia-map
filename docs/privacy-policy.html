Carryolia – Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Carryolia is a strategy game played on the real map, made by Rob van den Bekerom (the Netherlands). This policy explains what happens with your data when you play. In short: the game has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and your games stay on your phone. The game does not use your phone's location. To show the map, it asks a few map services for data about the area you play in.

1. Location

The game does not ask for, use or store your phone's location. You choose where to play by tapping the map.

2. Map services the game talks to

To draw the map and load the buildings, roads, forests and water where you play, the game requests data for the map area you are playing in. These requests go directly from your phone to the following services. Like any web request, they include your IP address and the requested map area.

ServiceWhat forOperator
GitHub (github.com, release downloads)Buildings, roads, nature in the game: pre-built map data files, downloaded in pieces for the area you play inGitHub, Inc. (Microsoft), USA
OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org)Background map imagesOpenFreeMap, via Cloudflare

I have no access to what these services log. Their own privacy policies apply. The game does not send them any identifier other than what every web request contains.

3. Data stored on your phone

Your saved games, settings (music volume, display options) and downloaded map data are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. If Android backup is on, your saved games and settings may be included in your Google account's device backup (downloaded map data is excluded). Uninstalling the game deletes all of it.

4. What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in, no name or e-mail address asked.
  • No advertising, no advertising ID.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

5. Feedback

If you e-mail me (for example about the game, or with feedback), I only receive what you choose to send, and use it only to answer you and to improve the game.

Google Play may share crash reports and app performance data with me if you have allowed this in your phone's settings; these do not identify you.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to ask what personal data I hold about you and to have it removed. Apart from e-mails you sent me yourself, I hold none. Questions: Carryolia@hotmail.com.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
